    Compliance copilot for trade finance (concept)

    AI product idea

    Problem

    Ops teams read long trade documents by hand to check them against compliance rules.

    Concept

    An LLM assistant that categorise documents and extracts fields, flags mismatches and cites the rule behind each flag, perform sanction screening, give risk score and the final decision, A human verfiies decision, initate payments.

    Success metrics

    Review time per document, false-flag rate and share of flags accepted by reviewers. This is a sample concept, not a shipped product.
